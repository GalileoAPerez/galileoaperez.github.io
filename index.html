MIAMI FORMULA 1 GRAND PRIX

Of the 3 grand prix in the United States, the Miami Grand Prix might be the least exciting race of them all. Long strethes around large bodies of water does lead to nice pictures and videos, but it results in boring races. The only hope for this track itself is rain, which is predicted to happen on race day, May 4th. Crashes, racecraft, and a new winner could result if this is the case, making this race way better than the snooze fest that was the Suzuka Grand Prix. This links to the race track information.
